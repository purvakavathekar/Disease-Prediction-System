1. Executive Summary & Model Accuracy Verification

To avoid artificial 100% textbook memorization, the models were evaluated under Method 1 (Simulated Clinical Noise & Symptom Dropout), which simulates real-world patient ambiguity where 25%–35% of symptoms are omitted and non-specific symptoms are reported.
